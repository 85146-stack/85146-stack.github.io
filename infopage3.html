Introduction to World War 1

World War 1:

WWI was one of the major world wars that took place between the years 1914 and 1918. In this war, there were two sides involved. The first one is referred to as the Allied Powers, which include nations such as Britain, France, Russia, and even the US, and the second one is called the Central Powers, including nations like Germany, Austria-Hungary, and the Ottoman Empire. The immediate cause of WWI can be traced back to the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria-Hungary on 28 June 1914. But the truth is that there had been various tensions among the nations of Europe because of various reasons such as alliances, power struggles, nationalism, and military expansion. WWI started in Europe, but then it spread across all nations of the world. Millions of lives were lost in this war, and resources were destroyed too. Another major economic impact of WWI was that the government had to spend millions of dollars on weapons, soldiers, and other items.

Economic Damage Caused by World War I

Economic effects of World War I include serious economic losses for many countries. The factories have started making military equipment, while millions of people went to the battlefields, which decreased the workforce available to make ordinary consumer goods. Large sums of money have been used to finance the war by many governments. Countries such as Britain, France and Germany have taken many loans to finance the war. The governments have got serious debt after World War One. The war also damaged the land, factories, railways, roads and other infrastructure of many regions, especially in France and Belgium, which meant that they needed much money for restoration. One more serious economic problem of that time was inflation. Many governments spent much money and produced few goods, which led to prices increasing in many countries. Particularly, Germany faced such economic problems after the war. The economic balance has changed due to the war. Europe used to be an economic power before the war; however, due to World War One America started to become more and more economically powerful.
