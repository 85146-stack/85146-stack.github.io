Introduction to Gaza vs Israel War

The war between Israel and Palestinians can be regarded as an ongoing war due to political, territorial, security, and historical differences. The latest war began due to the attacks of Hamas on Israel on 7 October 2023. The consequences of the war have been extremely disastrous for civilians, infrastructure, business, and the economy. Gaza was hit by the destruction of housing and business properties, roads, hospitals, schools, and water supplies. The cost of damage and necessary reconstruction work for the country was evaluated by the World Bank, the United Nations, and the European Union. The war also affected Israel's economy. The economic impact includes military mobilization, additional expenditure on defense, problems with business activities, a drop in tourists, and a shortage of the labor force. According to the reports of the IMF, the conflict caused serious economic difficulties for Israel, but the economy of the country proved its resilience.

IMF Report on the Economic Impact of the Conflict

Economic Damage Caused by the War within Israel and Gaza:

The economic damage in Gaza has been extremely large. Buildings and infrastructure have been destroyed, and many businesses and workplaces have been unable to operate. The World Bank estimates that Gaza's physical capital damages reached approximately US$35.2 billion by October 2025. Gaza's economy also suffered a huge reduction in its economic activity. In 2024, Gaza's real GDP went down by about 83%, which shows how severely the war wth Israel has disrupted businesses, production, trade, and employment for the country. The cost of rebuilding Gaza’s main buildings is expected to be enormous. A 2026 assessment by the World Bank, United Nations, and the European Union estimated the total recovery and reconstruction that the country needs will be about US$71.5 billion.

The Impact on Other Countries:

The implications of the conflict have transcended the borders of Israel and Gaza. There has been economic instability in other Middle Eastern countries due to the risk of conflict spreading to other regions. There has also been an impact on energy markets due to conflict and instability in the Middle East. According to the IMF, the effect of conflict in the area includes high energy prices, disruptions in the supply chain, and economic uncertainty. High energy prices increase costs related to transportation and production of goods. This can lead to inflation in nations that are heavily dependent on imported energy. There is also an effect on tourism and trade. The nations located near conflict areas will receive fewer tourists due to the danger factor. There will be an increased cost of insurance, transport, and operations of businesses. As per the IMF, there can be severe economic implications for the nations depending on imported energy or having scarce financial resources.

Economic Impact of the Gaza War

Gaza There has been the greatest direct economic damage in Gaza, where large portions of infrastructure and productive capacity have been destroyed, and many enterprises have ceased operations. The World Bank believes that about US$71.5 billion is required for Gaza to rebuild and restore itself.

Israel The economy of Israel has suffered due to increased defense expenditure, military mobilization, labor shortage, and economic disruption. As per the IMF, the economy had contracted significantly after the October 2023 conflict before starting to rebound.

Other Countries The conflict has had economic implications for other countries, especially those in the Middle East. The IMF has highlighted that the war has led to increased energy prices, disruptions in trade and supply chains, and economic uncertainty in the region.

IMF Analysis on Middle East Conflict and Economic Impact

What can be learned from this conflict:

In particular, the Gaza-Israel conflict has shown that wars can have massive economic impacts on the countries involved. Gaza has had a huge destruction of its infrastructure, businesses, homes, and offices, whereas Israel has had additional defense expenditures, loss of labour, and interference in its economic activity. Moreover, the negative economic impacts could be observed in other countries owing to changing energy prices, decrease in tourism, commerce, investment, and disruptions in the supply chain of the countries. It proves the interdependence of economies in the modern world. One of the key economic lessons from the war is that the cost of wars goes beyond the periods of high combat activity. In order to reconstruct infrastructure, rebuild businesses, create jobs, and restore the services, it takes a lot of time and billions of dollars. The cost of Gaza's recovery and reconstruction estimated by the World Bank for 2026 is estimated at about $71.5 billion. In general, wars can have both direct economic impacts such as destruction, defense expenditures, and indirect impacts like unemployment, inflation, lower commerce, and investment.
